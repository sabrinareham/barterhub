About Barter Hub

Many people have things at home that they no longer use. Other people need those same things. Most websites ask you to buy or sell, which needs money.

Barter Hub is a simple place to exchange items directly. You list what you have, say what you want, and the system suggests people who match.

What you can do
  • List items with photos, condition and the item you want in return.
  • Search and filter items by category, city and condition.
  • Get suggested matches, send requests, make counter offers and chat.
  • Complete the exchange and rate the other person.

The basic exchange service is free. Optional paid features will come later.

Barter Hub - exchange what you do not need for what you do. No buying, no selling.
