Terms and conditions

1. Exchange only

Barter Hub is for exchanging items. Selling for money is not allowed.

2. Honest listings

Describe your item truthfully, with real photos and the correct condition.

3. Forbidden items

Weapons, drugs, stolen goods, animals and any illegal items are not allowed.

4. Respect

Be polite in chat. Abusive users can be blocked by the admin.

5. Meeting safely

Meet in a public place. Check the item before you complete the exchange.

6. Moderation

To keep the site safe, the admin can see activity such as logins, searches and chat messages about swaps.
